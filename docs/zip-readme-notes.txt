Its settings are in the Mods panel (pause, "mods", Auto Pickup) and apply at once: what it collects, how far
around you, which items it leaves for you (weapons, parts and circuits; keys and mission items), how many med and
stasis packs it collects, and the pickup list in the bottom-right corner. With "Collect items automatically" off,
only a real Ammo Sweeper collects, as in the unmodded game.

Auto Pickup writes its lines to ds3_mods.log in the game folder. Attach that file to a bug report.
